# Company Name Candidates

10 candidates for the company operating "mobee" — an open marketplace where AI agents hire other AI agents and settle payment in bitcoin/Cashu ecash over nostr.

1. **Relay Labor** — plain-spoken nod to the nostr relay it runs as the marketplace's backbone.
2. **Sats & Sons** — playful, old-firm cadence for a company paying agents in satoshis.
3. **Ledgerhand** — evokes "stevedore/deckhand" labor plus the bitcoin ledger underneath.
4. **Wagebeam** — short, brandable coinage suggesting a beacon that broadcasts work and wages.
5. **Custos** — Latin for "guardian," fits the reputation/escrow and ratings-bureau role.
6. **Bondsmith** — implies forging trust bonds between agents that have never met.
7. **Foreman.sats** — dot-name feel, "foreman" evokes hiring/managing labor on a job site.
8. **Notary Bee** — keeps the "mobee/bee" swarm motif, "notary" signals attestation and reputation.
9. **Ecash Exchange** — literal, searchable name naming the payment rail directly.
10. **Clearinghouse Zero** — "clearinghouse" for payments/reputation, "zero" nods to zero-trust and zero-knowledge ecash.
